wger (https://gym.${DOMAIN}): log in as admin, password:
  lab secret wger WGER_ADMIN_PASSWORD
  (change it, and your email, under Settings; registration is off).
  The first start takes a few minutes (database, exercise list). Exercise
  images and the ingredient database arrive with the weekly sync; to get
  them now: lab compose wger exec web python3 manage.py download-exercise-images
Phone: the wger app (Play Store, F-Droid, App Store), "custom server"
  https://gym.${DOMAIN}; the phone needs to reach the server (VPN). Offline
  mode syncs through the same address (/ps/).

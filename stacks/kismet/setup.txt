Kismet (https://kismet.${DOMAIN}, user "kismet", ./lab secret kismet KISMET_PASSWORD):
  a Wi-Fi survey on a spare USB Wi-Fi adapter that can do monitor mode (MediaTek
  MT7612U/MT7921AU or Realtek RTL8821AU work with the kernel's own drivers). Name it
  with ./lab config kismet KISMET_SOURCE <interface> (ip -br link). Kismet only ever
  sees that adapter: built-in cards and one this machine is online through are
  refused. It runs on demand: ./lab up kismet starts it (plain ./lab up doesn't),
  ./lab down kismet stops it and gives the adapter back. Logs (./data, no data
  frames) are kept KISMET_KEEP_DAYS days and not backed up. They also list
  neighbours' networks and devices: keep them private.

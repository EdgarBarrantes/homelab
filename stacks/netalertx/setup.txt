NetAlertX (https://devices.${DOMAIN}, ./lab secret netalertx NETALERTX_PASSWORD):
  lists every device on the LAN (ARP scans of NET_IFACE's subnet every 5 minutes)
  and pushes new ones to ntfy (homelab-alerts) when the ntfy stack is enabled. The
  first scans report every device already there as new: name them in the UI
  (Devices), and tick "Alert down" on the ones you want to hear about when they go
  missing. Its login, scanned subnet and notifications come from the homelab and
  are re-applied at every start (settings.py); change others in its UI.
